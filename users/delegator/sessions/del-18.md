### Doorbell practice, step by step

Do this in short sessions, a few minutes, two or three times a day, when Biscuit is relaxed and a little hungry.

1. **Set up.** Have a pot of tiny, soft treats within reach. Stand a few steps away from the door.
2. **Quiet bell.** Have a friend (or a recording at low volume) ring once, as softly as you can. The instant the sound happens, say "yes" and give three or four treats in a row. Do it before he can bark.
3. **Repeat.** Do about five rings per session with pauses of a few seconds in between. The bell predicts treats, and the barking has no job to do.
4. **Build up.** Over several days, make it louder, then add a knock, then add the letterbox flap being lifted, then footsteps on the path.
5. **If he barks at any step,** you went up too fast. Drop back a step and make it easier. That's information, not a failure.

Once the bell reliably makes him look at you for treats, you've got the early gap you wanted, because he'll look to you the moment he hears something.
