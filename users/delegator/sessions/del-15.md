## Assistant

Fair worries, and both have simple answers.

**Fridge times**

For cooked vegetarian food kept cold, a sensible rule is three to four days. Cooked on Sunday evening, you're fine through Wednesday and, if the fridge is properly cold, into Thursday. Past that, freeze.

Rice is the one with a real rule, because of a bacterium whose spores survive cooking and then grow if rice sits warm. The fix is speed:

- Cool the rice within an hour. Spread it on a plate or the lid of the pot rather than leaving it in the rice cooker.
- Box it and get it in the fridge straight after.
- Eat it within a day or two, and only reheat it once, until it's steaming hot all the way through.

Because your plan has rice in dinners on Monday, Friday and in a couple of lunches, I'd cook two smaller batches: one for the first half of the week, and portion the second batch straight into the freezer. Frozen rice reheats well in the microwave with a splash of water.

For the rest:
- Dal and chilli: three to four days in the fridge, and both improve a day later.
- Roasted veg and grain: three days is best, so Monday and Wednesday are right.
- Boiled eggs, in their shells: up to a week, so Friday is fine.
- Pickled onions: weeks.
- Dressings: tahini-lemon about five days, the sesame one similar. Keep both in jars.
- Cooked noodles tossed in oil: about three days, which is why Thursday morning is the sensible time to dress them. If you're not sure on the day, smell them and look; slimy means bin.

**Freezing so you don't get a brick**

1. **Cool first.** Never put hot food in the freezer. Leave the pot off the heat for 30 to 40 minutes, then in the fridge for an hour if you can.
2. **Freeze flat in bags.** Ladle one portion (about 350 to 400ml) into a freezer bag, squeeze the air out, seal, and lay it flat on a tray or plate in the freezer. Once frozen, the bags stand upright like files, and they thaw in minutes because they're thin.
3. **Tubs work too,** but leave a gap at the top, since liquid expands. Leave one inch.
4. **Label with a marker:** dish and date. You think you'll remember. You won't.
5. **Thaw in the fridge** overnight, then reheat on the hob or in the microwave. Frozen-to-hot in a pan with a splash of water also works for the thin flat bags.

For your plan, that means: two portions of dal in bags (Thursday dinner, plus one spare), two of chilli (Friday dinner, plus one spare), and a portion or two of rice. Your freezer drawers will manage that easily.
